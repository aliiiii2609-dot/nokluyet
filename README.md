# nokluyet · the jellyverse 🍮

The softest corner of the internet. A soft-body physics jelly you can poke, drag, yeet and befriend.

## What's inside

- **Splash screen**: fake loading lines ("injecting rizz…") and a bass-drop entrance
- **The jelly**: real spring-and-pressure soft-body physics, eyes that track your cursor, blinks, happy squints, idle shivers
- **Catch 'Em**: emoji fruit rain, golden star fruit worth 5, chilis to dodge, combo tiers up to ABSOLUTE CINEMA
- **Wobble Rush**: 20 seconds of pure poking, combo counter, screen shake, meme ranks
- **5 flavors**: strawberry, mango, lychee, blueberry, matcha; recolors the whole world
- **10 achievements**: persisted in localStorage, from "first poke" to "absolute unit"
- **Secrets**: click the logo 7 times
- **Extras**: name your jelly, wobble-bass background music, emoji confetti, share button

## Run

It's one `index.html`. Open it, or serve the folder with anything.

## Stack

Vanilla JS + Canvas + WebAudio. Zero dependencies, zero build step, zero thoughts (just wobble).
